Frequently asked questions

How do I reset progress?

Open Progress, scroll to Reset Progress, and confirm Reset. This clears your on-device training progress.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad running iOS/iPadOS 17 or later.

Is it free?

Yes. No account, ads, or subscription is required.

Does it need internet?

Lessons, questions, explanations, and progress work offline. Live e-Pubs needs internet to check or download publications. External reference links also need internet. Saved PDFs can be read offline in Files.

Why does e-Pubs say “Open to Verify”?

The government host or your network may require a browser. Open the official publication and check its title page, changes, guidance memoranda, supplements, and local procedures. A successful reachability check alone does not establish publication currency.

Is this an official app?

This app is not an official Department of the Air Force product. It is a supplemental training aid intended to reinforce published guidance and OSHA standards. Always follow unit-specific procedures and the most current official publications.
