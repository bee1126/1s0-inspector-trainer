How do I switch tracks?

Tap the training-track chip on Today, or open Profile and select Training track. Choose OSHA / Civilian or Air Force (1S0), then confirm. Your XP, streak, bookmarks, and progress stay saved. Unavailable content is hidden until you switch back.

Does it issue OSHA cards or certification?

No. SafetyFluent is an independent study aid, not an OSHA Outreach Training Program course, and does not issue OSHA 10/30 cards or any certification.

How do I save or share a hazard report?

Open Practice → Hazard Report & Risk Matrix. Enter the location and hazard, assess risk, record controls, and select Save report. Share plain text or prepare a PDF and use Share PDF. Sharing does not submit a regulatory report.

Can I use it offline?

Yes, bundled lessons, questions, explanations, and local reports work offline. Opening official references requires a connection.

Why is my unfinished session hidden?

A track switch may hide some questions in the session. Switch back to resume it, or choose Restart in this track to begin with the visible question pool.
