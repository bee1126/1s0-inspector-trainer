SafetyXP Support Privacy

Privacy

Effective October 10, 2026

Data Not Collected

SafetyXP has no analytics, ads, or third-party SDKs. No account is required. The developer does not collect your training progress or hazard reports.

On-device storage

Training progress is saved on your device. Hazard reports are stored separately in the app's local Application Support folder. They can include locations, descriptions, owners, and corrective actions that you enter. Delete individual reports in the tool or use Delete all reports in Settings.

Sharing and connections

Reports leave the app only when you choose to export and share plain text or PDF. The destination you choose controls its copy. Reference links connect when tapped. The Air Force track also offers Live DAF e-Pubs, which contacts official publication services when opened or refreshed. The OSHA track does not load that service.

Device backups

Your operating system may include app data in device backups, according to your device settings. The developer has no access to those backups.
