Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026. Developer: Abdoul Bah (individual developer).

Data Not Collected

No account, no ads, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs. The developer collects no data through the app, including from children. The App Store privacy label is “Data Not Collected.”

On-device storage

Training progress, bookmarks, saved sessions, session history, preferences, watched publications, and revision-check metadata stay on your device. Reset training progress with Progress → Reset Progress → Reset. Delete the app to remove its local app data. Delete exported PDFs separately in Files.

Network access and official publications

The app's own network calls go only to official Department of the Air Force e-Publishing when you use Live e-Pubs to check or download publications. These government services may receive your IP address, request time, and standard network headers. The developer does not receive their server logs.

Other external reference links open in your browser. Their websites operate under their own privacy policies.

Offline PDFs

Save to Files for Offline Use downloads the selected official PDF and opens Apple's file exporter. You choose its storage location. The app does not upload the document or your selected location.

Sharing and support

Bug Report and Feature Request forms share the information you enter only when you choose a destination in Apple's share sheet. Report Broken Publication Link opens an email draft containing the publication number and title, official URL, app reachability status, and last-check time. It is sent only if you send it through your mail app. Support correspondence you choose to send is received by the developer; training progress is not automatically transmitted.
