Safety training put into practice.

SafetyFluent is a free training app for workplace safety. It turns OSHA general industry standards into quick lessons, real scenarios, and quizzes, with XP and streaks that keep you coming back.

Two tracks

OSHA / Civilian

15 modules · 150 questions

General industry training based on 29 CFR 1910 and 1904, with specific references and explanations.

Air Force (1S0)

19 modules · 190 questions

OSHA topics plus Air Force programs, ORM, mishap reporting, and DAF field exercises.

What’s inside

Daily Five, spaced review, custom study and exams, saved questions, resumable sessions, and a topic-by-topic debrief. Keep your XP, streak, and progress when switching tracks.

Tools that stay with you

Hazard Report & Risk Matrix, PPE decisions, glossary, and regulation lookup. Save hazard reports locally and share text or PDF when you choose. The example risk matrix is not prescribed by OSHA.

Private by design

No account, analytics, ads, or third-party SDKs. Data Not Collected. Lessons and explanations work offline; reference links require internet. iOS 17 and later.

Built for everyday learning

Built by Abdoul Bah, an independent developer with occupational safety experience who wanted a better way to keep safety knowledge fresh and ready to use.
