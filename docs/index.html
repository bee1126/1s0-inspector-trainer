1S0 Inspector Trainer

Interactive safety training for Air Force inspectors. Free for iPhone and iPad, iOS 17 or later.

This app is not an official Department of the Air Force product. It is a supplemental training aid intended to reinforce published guidance and OSHA standards. Always follow unit-specific procedures and the most current official publications.

Download on the App Store

Version 1.7: more ways to study

  • 14 safety modules and 140 questions with offline explanations and official references.
  • Custom study and exam sessions by topic, difficulty, length, and saved, missed, or due questions; scored debriefs by topic.
  • Bookmarks, resumable sessions, session history, and a searchable module catalog.
  • Daily Five adaptive review and a 7-day onboarding path.
  • Field tools: PPE loadouts, DAF Form 457 hazard reporting and RAC practice, Deployed ORM, and regulation lookup.
  • Live DAF e-Pubs: check publication revisions and save official PDFs to Files for offline use.
  • Glossary, XP, levels, streaks, badges, and a daily XP goal.

Five tabs: Today, Learn, Practice, Library, and Progress. No account, ads, analytics, or third-party SDKs. Your progress stays on your device.
