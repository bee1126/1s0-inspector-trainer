## Website (docs/index.html)
Headline: Safety training put into practice.
Subhead: SafetyFluent is a free training app for workplace safety. It turns OSHA general industry standards into quick lessons, real scenarios, and quizzes, with XP and streaks that keep you coming back.
CTA button: Download on the App Store (link above)
Section, Two tracks:
- OSHA / Civilian: 15 modules and 150 questions on general industry (29 CFR 1910) and recordkeeping (29 CFR 1904), citing OSHA standards only.
- Air Force (1S0): 19 modules and 190 questions covering OSHA topics plus Air Force safety programs and field tools.
Section, What's inside:
- Up to 19 modules and 190 questions per track, each with an explanation and its official reference
- Custom study and exam sessions with a scored, topic-by-topic debrief
- Daily Five spaced review, bookmarks, and session history
- Field tools: hazard report with risk scoring, PPE loadout, regulation lookup
Section, Private by design: No account, no ads, no analytics. Your progress stays on your device.
Section, Built for everyday learning: SafetyFluent was built by Abdoul Bah, an independent developer with occupational safety experience who wanted a better way to keep safety knowledge fresh and ready to use.
Footer disclaimer: SafetyFluent is an independent study aid made by an individual developer. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official product of the Department of the Air Force, the U.S. Air Force, the Department of Defense, OSHA or the U.S. Department of Labor, or ISO. It is not an OSHA Outreach Training Program course and does not issue OSHA 10/30 cards or any certification. Content paraphrases public standards and publications; always follow your employer's or unit's procedures and the most current official text.
Note: the support and privacy pages only need the name swapped to SafetyFluent, plus a line saying it was formerly 1S0 Inspector Trainer.
