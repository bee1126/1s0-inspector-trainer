## Description
Safety training put into practice, a few minutes a day. SafetyFluent turns OSHA general industry standards into short lessons, real-world scenarios, and quizzes with XP, streaks, and levels. Built by an independent developer with occupational safety experience, it works for safety pros, students, and supervisors everywhere.

PICK YOUR TRACK
• OSHA / Civilian: 15 modules and 150 questions on general industry (29 CFR 1910) and recordkeeping (29 CFR 1904), citing OSHA standards only
• Air Force (1S0): 19 modules and 190 questions covering OSHA topics plus Air Force safety programs, deployed ORM, and the hazard report and RAC tools
• Switch anytime, and your progress carries over

LEARN
• Up to 19 modules, including Lockout/Tagout, Fall Protection, Walking-Working Surfaces, Confined Space, Hazard Communication, Electrical Safety, Machine Guarding, Material Handling, Fire/Hot Work, PPE Hazard Assessment, Respiratory Protection, Hearing Conservation, Recordkeeping, and Emergency Action Plans
• Scenario decision paths with instant feedback
• Daily Five: adaptive spaced review that brings back what you're about to forget

PRACTICE AND TEST
• Up to 190 questions per track, each with an explanation and its official reference, all available offline
• Custom study and exam sessions: pick the topic, difficulty, length, and question pool (saved, missed, or due)
• Exam mode with a scored debrief broken down by topic
• Bookmarks, resumable sessions, and session history

FIELD TOOLS AND LIBRARY
• Hazard report with risk scoring
• PPE loadout builder
• Regulation lookup and glossary
• Official publication links, with PDFs you can save for offline use

STAY MOTIVATED
• XP, levels, streaks, badges, and a daily XP goal
• A Progress tab that shows how you're doing across every topic

BUILT FOR PRIVACY
