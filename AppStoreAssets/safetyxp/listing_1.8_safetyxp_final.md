## Description
Stay inspection-ready in a few minutes a day. SafetyXP turns safety standards into short lessons, practical decisions, and quizzes with XP, streaks, and levels. Built by an independent developer for safety professionals, students, and supervisors.

PICK YOUR TRACK
• OSHA / Civilian: 15 modules and 150 questions covering general industry (29 CFR 1910) and recordkeeping (29 CFR 1904)
• Air Force (1S0): 19 modules and 190 questions, including Air Force safety programs, ORM, mishap reporting, and DAF field exercises
• Switch anytime; your XP, streak, and progress carry over

LEARN AND PRACTICE
• Five new modules: Walking-Working Surfaces, PPE Hazard Assessment, Recordkeeping, Emergency Action & Fire Prevention, and Respiratory Protection
• Lessons, decision scenarios, and explanations with official references
• Daily Five adaptive practice and spaced review
• Custom study and exams by topic, difficulty, length, and saved, missed, or due questions
• Scored debriefs, bookmarks, resumable sessions, and session history
• Bundled training content works offline; reference links require internet

FIELD TOOLS AND LIBRARY
• Hazard Report & Risk Matrix: keep local reports, record controls, and export text or PDF
• Example risk matrix; OSHA does not prescribe a risk matrix
• PPE decisions, regulation lookup, and glossary
• OSHA Standards links on the civilian track; additional DAF publication tools on the Air Force track

FREE AND PRIVATE
• No account, ads, analytics, or third-party SDKs
• Data Not Collected
• Progress and hazard reports stay on your device; export reports only when you choose
